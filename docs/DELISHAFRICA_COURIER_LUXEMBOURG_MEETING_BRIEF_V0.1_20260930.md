# DelishAfrica® Courier — Luxembourg Stakeholder Meeting Brief V0.1

Date: 30 September 2026  
Status: INTERNAL DRAFT  
Initial outreach targets: OGBL, LCGB

## Objective

Validate the human/legal realities of Courier in Luxembourg before selecting the final operating model.

## DelishAfrica already decided

- online/offline choice;
- explicit accept/decline;
- no refusal penalty in dispatch ranking;
- no current auto-assignment;
- advisory Route Oracle;
- no permanent deactivation solely by algorithm;
- human review + appeal baseline;
- no continuous off-duty tracking as product direction.

## Luxembourg-specific OPEN items

- employee vs independent model;
- establishment-authorisation requirements for delivery couriers;
- pay model;
- invoicing/tax;
- cross-border social-security handling;
- exact geolocation notice/retention;
- final platform-work transposition rules.

## 45-minute structure

### 0–8 min — Why DelishAfrica is consulting now
- Client / Merchant / Courier ecosystem;
- Merchant live;
- Courier pre-launch;
- no mass onboarding before framework validation.

### 8–20 min — Status / cross-border reality
Ask:
- what worker-status problems are most common in Luxembourg platform work?
- what controls would make an "independent" model unrealistic?
- how frequent are France/Belgium-resident courier cases?
- what should a platform explain about CCSS/A1 without giving individual legal advice?

### 20–30 min — Pay / operating costs
Ask:
- what cost items are routinely underestimated?
- what mission information should be visible before acceptance?
- waiting time?
- vehicle/phone/data costs?
- payment cadence?

### 30–38 min — Geolocation / algorithms
Present state split:
OFFLINE → VERIFY → ONLINE → MISSION → COMPLETE.

Ask:
- which tracking is unacceptable?
- what retention is proportionate?
- what dispatch information should be explainable?

### 38–43 min — Deactivation / appeal
Ask:
- expected notice;
- representation/support;
- human review;
- urgent suspension cases;
- reasonable appeal SLA.

### 43–45 min — Follow-up
- Luxembourg Annex V0.2;
- competent-authority questions;
- product requirements;
- legal review after transposition update.
