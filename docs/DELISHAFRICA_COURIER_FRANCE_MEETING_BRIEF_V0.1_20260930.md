# DelishAfrica® Courier — France Stakeholder Meeting Brief V0.1

Date: 30 September 2026  
Status: INTERNAL DRAFT  
Primary outreach target: Union-Indépendants

## Objective

Stress-test DelishAfrica's France Courier model before paid onboarding.

## DelishAfrica already decided

- explicit online/offline choice;
- explicit accept/decline;
- refusal does not lower dispatch ranking;
- no current automatic assignment;
- Route Oracle is advisory;
- no permanent deactivation solely by algorithm;
- human-review path for material decisions;
- country-specific public Courier framework.

## France-specific facts to acknowledge

- sector rules already govern deactivation;
- independent couriers covered by the 2026 amendment have a 19 €/hour-of-activity weekly income guarantee from 1 September 2026;
- tips are outside the guarantee base;
- structured access to own platform-activity data is a statutory topic;
- accident protection is a statutory platform-responsibility topic under applicable conditions.

## 45-minute meeting structure

### 0–8 min — Product + why we asked
- Client / Merchant / Courier;
- Merchant live;
- Courier preparing public launch;
- no mass onboarding before country framework is ready.

### 8–20 min — Pay
Ask:
- how should the 19 € guarantee be shown in-app so it is actually understandable?
- which waiting-time events are most contentious?
- should weekly guarantee progress be visible live?
- what should count as a separate bonus versus covered income?
- which pay statement fields are indispensable?

### 20–32 min — Deactivation
Present state-machine concept and ask:
- what evidence should be visible to the courier?
- preferred appeal channel?
- how should protective suspension be explained?
- what should the app show during the 24h/48h procedural windows?
- what accessibility/language issues are commonly missed?

### 32–40 min — Data / dispatch
Explain:
- acceptance rate removed from ranking;
- current factors include ETA, load, reliability, location freshness and fairness;
- human/courier confirmation required.

Ask:
- which factors should always be disclosed?
- what should the courier be able to export?
- what scoring should never be used?

### 40–45 min — Follow-up
- revise France Annex V0.2;
- convert agreed points into product requirements;
- maintain representative dialogue before onboarding.

## Do not improvise

Do not state a final:
- worker status;
- per-mission rate;
- waiting-time rate;
- insurance product;
- tax/invoicing model;
- ARPE charter strategy.
