# DelishAfrica® Courier — Cameroon Stakeholder Meeting Brief V0.1

Date: 30 September 2026  
Status: INTERNAL DRAFT

## Objective

Validate the real field, transport, social-protection and data constraints of Courier in Cameroon before paid onboarding.

## DelishAfrica already decided

- explicit online/offline choice;
- explicit accept/decline;
- refusal does not lower dispatch ranking;
- no current auto-assignment;
- Route Oracle is advisory;
- no permanent deactivation solely by algorithm;
- human review + appeal baseline;
- no continuous off-duty tracking as product direction;
- country framework visible before activation.

## Cameroon-specific OPEN items

- employee vs independent model;
- road-transport classification for food/parcel delivery;
- motorcycle/bicycle/car permit and insurance requirements;
- platform classification as transport organiser/messenger service;
- accident/injury protection for independent couriers;
- tax/IGS classification;
- compensation/payment model;
- Law 2024/017 operational compliance/authority process.

## 45-minute field meeting structure

### 0–8 min — Why DelishAfrica is consulting now
- Client / Merchant / Courier ecosystem;
- Merchant live;
- Courier pre-launch;
- no mass onboarding before the local framework is coherent.

### 8–18 min — Work model / economics
Ask:
- what costs make delivery activity unviable in practice?
- what should be visible before accepting a mission?
- how are fuel, maintenance, mobile data and equipment handled in the field?
- what payment frequency is workable?
- what causes the biggest disputes with platforms?

### 18–28 min — Vehicle / safety / transport rules
Ask:
- which documents are actually requested for motorcycle delivery?
- difference between food delivery and passenger moto-taxi practice?
- municipal zone/identifier issues?
- insurance reality?
- accident handling?
- minimum safety equipment?

### 28–36 min — CNPS / protection
Ask:
- how do riders currently approach CNPS?
- what protection gaps exist for independent riders?
- what accident/health protection would materially improve trust?

### 36–42 min — Data / geolocation / account decisions
Explain:
- acceptance rate removed from ranking;
- location state-machine;
- human deactivation review.

Ask:
- what tracking feels excessive?
- what should a rider be able to see/export?
- what evidence should be provided before suspension/deactivation?

### 42–45 min — Follow-up
- Cameroon Annex V0.2;
- competent-authority questions;
- product requirements;
- local legal/data review.

## Do not improvise

Do not state:
- final worker status;
- that the S2 moto-taxi regime definitely applies to delivery;
- a final tax regime;
- final pay/rate;
- that voluntary CNPS equals employee accident/social coverage;
- that the personal-data authority process is already fully operational.
