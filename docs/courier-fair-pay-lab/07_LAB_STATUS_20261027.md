## Belgium scenario runner

Illustrative assumptions only:
- employer courier cost: 30 EUR/paid hour;
- contribution before courier: 12 EUR/delivery;
- target residual: 2 EUR/delivery.

Outputs:
- LOW: 1.5 deliveries/h, utilization 50%, residual -120 EUR over 10 paid hours;
- BASE: 2.5 deliveries/h, utilization 75%, break-even;
- HIGH: 3.0 deliveries/h, utilization 85%, residual +60 EUR over 10 paid hours.

The worker pay remains protected in every scenario.
